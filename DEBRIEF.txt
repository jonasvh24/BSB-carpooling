1. WHAT THIS IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
---------------------------------
Poolmate is a carpooling web app for the staff of one school. Teachers and
other staff sign in with their school email, say where they live and what
hours they work, and the app finds colleagues who live along the same route
and keep compatible hours. Matched people can form a carpool ("pool"), get a
daily "tomorrow's pickup is at 07:42" message, confirm or cancel with one
tap, and log that the trip happened. The school's pilot owner gets an admin
dashboard that says whether the idea actually works at this school.

It is a PILOT tool, not a finished product. It is meant to run for six weeks
on one laptop and answer three questions, in this order:

  1. Density    - do enough staff live near each other, with compatible
                  hours, to be matched at all?
  2. Behaviour  - do matches turn into carpools that are still running after
                  week 4?
  3. Commercial - would the school or an employer pay for this, and for what?

If question 1 fails, the pilot stops. The admin dashboard is built to show
that answer within days of launch.

Not included on purpose: students, parents, school trips, in-app payments,
app-store apps.

4. WHAT A STAFF MEMBER CAN DO
-----------------------------
Sign in
  Email magic link, no passwords. Only the school's email domains are
  allowed, plus addresses an admin has invited.

Onboarding (seven short screens, built for a phone, under five minutes)
  1. About you       - name, optional phone and photo, languages
  2. Where you live  - address search with a map pin you can drag
  3. School days     - which days, arrive-by time, leave time, how flexible;
                       per-day grid for part-timers and multi-campus staff
  4. Drive or ride   - driver / passenger / either; car, seats, max detour;
                       max walk and max extra time for passengers
  5. Preferences     - smoking, quiet or chatty, music, same-gender option,
                       how to share costs, a short note
  6. Research        - ten pilot questions (how you commute now, what it
                       costs, would you pay, etc.)
  7. Consent         - two required checkboxes (location sharing, research)
                       and the ground rules

Matches (/matches)
  A list of compatible colleagues with a score out of 100 and plain reasons
  such as "+6 min detour", "Both at school by 08:10 on Wed, Fri",
  "Pickup 08:03 at your door", "You both speak Dutch". Morning and afternoon
  are matched separately. Buttons: "Ask to join" / "Invite" / "Not for me".
  If nobody matches yet, it shows how many colleagues live nearby (counts
  only, never names) and an invite link.

Requests (/requests)
  Accept, decline or withdraw. Requests expire after seven days.

Carpools (/pools and /pools/[id])
  Members, a map with the route and stops, a per-weekday timetable (driver
  leaves, each pickup, arrival), chat, upcoming trips, "I can't make it
  on...", leave, report a problem. Drivers can also set a meeting point,
  change days, pause the pool and turn on driver rotation (the driver
  changes each week).

Daily running
  18:00  - tomorrow's trips are created and everyone gets a digest with
           Confirm / Can't make it.
  If the driver cancels - everyone is told at once, and passengers get a
           "Find a lift" list of other drivers plus a public transport link.
  20:00  - the driver is asked whether the trip happened.
  After 72 hours with no answer the trip is marked done (unverified).
  02:00  - all matches are recomputed and old trip logs are anonymised.

Also: cost-sharing tally per pool (settled outside the app), reliability
score after five trips, thumbs up after a trip, notifications page, profile
and schedule editing, week-4 and exit surveys.

Settings and privacy (/settings, /settings/privacy)
  Mute email, see and withdraw consents, download all your data as JSON,
  delete your account, report or block a colleague.


5. WHAT THE ADMIN CAN DO (/admin)
---------------------------------
Dashboard with every metric the brief asks for, each with its definition:
sign-up rate, onboarding completion, role and site split, how many people
are "matchable", why the rest are not (nobody in the corridor, times don't
fit, no drivers, no seats), pools, trips per week, cancellation and no-show
rates, km saved, CO2 saved, parking spaces freed, money saved, survey
results as bar charts, and a heat map of where staff live (coarse cells
only, never a single household).

Go/no-go panel: the pilot's pass marks (for example sign-up rate at least
30%, matchable at least 50%) shown against the current values, colour coded.
The thresholds can be edited.

Actions: invite staff by pasting a CSV of emails, manage sites, holiday
calendar, rates and headcount, recompute matches, export CSV (users,
matches, trips, survey), suspend or delete a user, work through reports,
send the week-4 and exit surveys. Every admin action is written to an audit
log.


6. HOW MATCHING WORKS (SHORT VERSION)
-------------------------------------
For each site, weekday and direction (morning = home to school, afternoon =
school to home) it pairs drivers with passengers. A pair is thrown out if
they share no days, conflict on smoking, fail the same-gender preference,
the passenger is not roughly on the driver's way, the real routed detour is
longer than the driver allows, their time windows do not overlap, or the car
is full.

Survivors get a score:
  40% how small the detour is
  25% how close their times are
  15% how many days they share
  10% soft preferences (quiet vs chatty, shared language)
  10% reliability from past trips
Only scores of 40 or more are shown.

Driving times come from free OpenStreetMap services (OSRM). If that service
is down, the app falls back to a straight-line estimate and labels times as
estimates. No paid APIs and no API keys anywhere.


7. PRIVACY RULES BUILT IN
-------------------------
Home addresses are personal data under GDPR, so these are hard rules:
  - Staff only. No minors anywhere in the system.
  - Your exact home is visible only to you and members of a pool you have
    joined. Everyone else sees an approximate area of about 500 m.
  - Phone numbers are shown only inside an accepted pool.
  - Gender is stored only if you opt into same-gender matching, and is never
    displayed.
  - Consent is recorded with a policy version. A new policy version forces
    everyone to consent again. Withdrawing location consent hides you from
    matching.
  - Delete account really deletes; trip history is anonymised.
  - Trip logs are anonymised 12 months after a pool ends.
  - Blocked pairs never match. Sign-in, search, requests, messages and
    reports are rate limited.
  - /privacy is a plain-language notice. It is a DRAFT for the pilot owner
    to edit before real use.
  - With PILOT_MODE=true every developer shortcut (/dev pages) is gone.


8. HOW IT IS BUILT
------------------
  Type        Mobile-first web app that installs to the phone home screen
              (PWA). One codebase, works on desktop too.
  Framework   Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4
  Database    SQLite file (prisma/dev.db) through Prisma 6. Written so it
              can move to Postgres later without a rewrite.
  Sign-in     Auth.js v5 email magic link
  Email       Nodemailer over SMTP. In development the link is printed to
              the console and shown at /dev/mailbox.
  Maps        Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles; Photon for address search;
              Nominatim for pin drags; OSRM for driving times
  Schedule    node-cron inside the app (18:00, 20:00, 02:00 jobs)
  Tests       Vitest (35 unit tests) and Playwright (9 browser tests)
  Language    English UI (British), every string in src/i18n/en.ts so Dutch
              and French can be added later as translation files
  Tenancy     Organisation -> sites -> users, so more schools or companies
              are added as data later

11. HOW TO USE THE PREVIEW
--------------------------
The preview is running now at http://localhost:3100

Fastest way to look around:
  1. Open http://localhost:3100/dev
  2. Click "login" next to any person to become them. Pick a seed user to
     see matches, or admin@example.org to see the admin dashboard at /admin.

To try the real sign-in: go to /signin, enter anything@example.org, then
open http://localhost:3100/dev/mailbox and click the link.

Port 3000 is used by another of your projects (kleer), which is why this one
is on 3100.

If the preview has stopped (for example after a restart of the Mac):
  cd ~/Projects/grey-tag/jvcarpool
  npx next dev -p 3100

Other commands:
  npm test              run all tests
  npm run seed          add the 40 fake staff
  npm run seed:purge    remove all fake staff
  npm run recompute     recompute all matches
  npm run backup        copy the database to backups/
  npm run tunnel        public link for phones (needs cloudflared)
