# Launch announcement

## Staffroom note (≈150 words)

**Share the drive to school — a six-week trial**

Many of us drive to school alone from the same streets. For the next six weeks we are trying Poolmate, a small app that finds colleagues who live near you and start and finish at similar times, and helps you share the drive on the days that suit you both.

It takes about five minutes on your phone: sign in with your school email, tell it where you live and when you need to be in, and it shows you who you could pair up with. You choose whether to drive, ride or take turns, one-way or both ways. Nothing is shared without your say-so: colleagues only see roughly where you live until you are in a carpool together, and your phone number stays private until then.

Please set it up this week even if you are not sure you will carpool. The trial only works if enough of us are in it to find matches. Scan the QR code or open the link below.

Questions: ask [pilot owner].
